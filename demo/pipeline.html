THE DELIVERY CONTRACT

From report to release.

One issue. One bounded attempt.
  1. 01

    Authorize

    A maintainer approves a complete bug report.

  2. 02

    Implement

    Codex proposes a focused patch in an isolated job.

  3. 03

    Prove

    The regression fails on the original, then passes on the fix.

  4. 04

    Review

    A fresh agent reviews the exact source and leaves findings.

  5. 05

    Decide

    A human reviews the evidence and merges the PR.

  6. 06

    Ship

    The tested build reaches Pages and its commit is verified.
